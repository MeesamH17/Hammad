How Ghostwriting Works

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We talk through your idea, your readers and what you want the book to achieve.

  2. 02

    Outline

    We agree the structure and the chapters before any writing begins.

  3. 03

    Drafting

    Your writer drafts the manuscript, drawing on your notes, interviews and ideas.

  4. 04

    Your feedback

    You read the draft and tell us what to change or add.

  5. 05

    Finished manuscript

    You receive a polished manuscript, ready for editing, design and publishing.
