Cookie Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website can store on your device. They are used to make sites work, remember settings and understand how sites are used.

What this website uses

At the time of writing, this website does not set analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not store information about you in your browser. Because of this, we do not show a cookie banner.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts. The website fonts are loaded from Google’s servers, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • Hosting. Our host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.
  • Enquiry form and links. If you submit the form or follow links to other sites, such as Amazon or WhatsApp, those services may set their own cookies under their own policies.

If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so may affect how some websites work. See aboutcookies.org for guidance.
