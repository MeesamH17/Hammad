Open index.html by double-clicking it. Upload the whole folder to GitHub Pages to publish.
Pages: index.html (homepage), publishing.html, ghostwriting.html, book-marketing.html, distribution.html, about.html, privacy.html, terms.html, cookies.html

BEFORE LAUNCH - edit js/site-data.js:
1. email, phone, location  (replace the [PLACEHOLDERS]; they also appear on the 3 legal pages)
2. formEndpoint            (free Formspree URL; leave empty to open the visitor's email app)
3. whatsapp                (digits with country code, e.g. 447123456789; empty hides the button)
4. socials                 (your real Facebook / Instagram / LinkedIn / X links)
5. books / childrenBooks   (title, author, genre, Amazon link, cover in assets/books)
6. testimonials            (set testimonialsReady = false to hide the section)

LEGAL PAGES: fill the [BRACKETS] in privacy.html, terms.html and cookies.html
([LEGAL COMPANY NAME], [COMPANY NUMBER], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], [FORM PROVIDER], [12 MONTHS])
and have a solicitor review them before launch.

Packages: index.html, "Publishing Solutions" section. Confirm the inclusions match what you really offer.
